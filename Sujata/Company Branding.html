Trademark Registration

Secure your brand identity, protect your logo and business name with trademark registration.

Why Trademark Registration?

A trademark ensures that your brand, logo, or slogan is legally protected from misuse or duplication. It builds credibility and trust with your customers.

  • Legal protection against brand misuse
  • Exclusive rights to use your logo/name
  • Boosts business credibility
  • Helps in expansion & franchising

Process of Trademark Registration

  1. ✅Trademark search
  2. ✅Filing application
  3. ✅Examination by authorities
  4. ✅Publication in journal
  5. ✅Approval & certification
