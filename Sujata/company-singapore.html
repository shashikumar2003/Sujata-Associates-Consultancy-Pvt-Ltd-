Company Registration in Singapore

Singapore offers one of the most business-friendly environments globally.

Why Register in Singapore?

Known for its tax benefits and ease of doing business, Singapore is an ideal hub for startups.

  • Low corporate tax rates
  • Full foreign ownership allowed
  • Strong banking and global reputation

Process

  1. Choose business structure (Pte. Ltd. preferred)
  2. Submit shareholder and director details
  3. File with ACRA
  4. Open Singapore corporate bank account
  5. Apply for GST registration if needed
