Why Register in UAE/Dubai?

Dubai is the gateway to the Gulf region with a tax-free environment in many zones.

  • 100% foreign ownership in free zones
  • No personal or corporate income tax

Process

  1. Select jurisdiction (Mainland / Free Zone / Offshore)
  2. Submit documents (Passport, Visa, Address proof)
  3. Name reservation and license application
  4. Obtain trade license
  5. Open corporate bank account
