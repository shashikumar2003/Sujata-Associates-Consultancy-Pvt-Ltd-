Expand your operations in one of South Asia’s fastest-growing economies.

Why Register in Bangladesh?

Bangladesh is a cost-effective market with opportunities in textiles, IT, and manufacturing.

  • Low incorporation cost
  • Fast-growing export economy
  • Supportive government policies

Process

  1. Reserve company name with RJSC
  2. Prepare Articles of Association
  3. File incorporation with RJSC
  4. Obtain TIN and VAT registration
  5. Open corporate bank account
