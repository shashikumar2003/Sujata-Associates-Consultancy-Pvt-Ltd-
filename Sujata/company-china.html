Establish your business presence in the world’s 2nd largest economy.

Why Register in China?

China offers massive growth potential in manufacturing and consumer markets.

  • Access to global supply chain
  • Large domestic market
  • Government incentives for foreign investors

Process

  1. Choose entity type (WFOE / JV / Representative Office)
  2. Reserve company name
  3. Submit incorporation documents
  4. Register with local Administration of Industry and Commerce (AIC)
  5. Apply for licenses and tax registration
